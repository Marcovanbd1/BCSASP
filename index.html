I have over two years' experience working within NHS Primary Care, combining healthcare, physical activity, service improvement and digital innovation to support people at every stage of ageing well.

Across five GP practices serving approximately 75,000 patients, I've worked at the intersection of preventative healthcare and exercise — designing falls management programmes, leading the rollout of remote monitoring technology, and building digital resources that connect patients with the right support at the right time.

I'm passionate about helping people become more active and independent through evidence-based exercise and preventative healthcare, and I work closely with GPs, nurses, physiotherapists, pharmacists and social prescribers to make that happen in practice, not just on paper.
